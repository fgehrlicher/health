# Estimated weights from photos

Use these only when he gave no weight. Mark every such amount `estimated: true`.
The numbers are rough judgement calls from typical sizes, not catalog values.
Nutrition still comes only from `health foods get`.

## Typical weights (rough, grams)

- Zucchini / courgette, whole, medium-long: about 200-250 g
- Tomato, large (beefsteak / standard): about 120-150 g
- Tomato, cherry or small vine: about 15-20 g each
- Orange, medium: about 150-180 g
- Apple, medium: about 150-180 g
- Banana, medium: about 110-120 g
- Egg, whole: about 50-60 g
- Single-serve yoghurt pot (Skyr, quark) at 450 g: use the package weight, not an estimate

## Method

1. Count the items you can see. Note the size against the visible objects
   (hand, plate, packaging).
2. Pick the typical weight for that size, then multiply by the count.
3. Say in the reply that the weight is a guess and give the range if it is wide.

## No scale, restaurant plate

When he has no way to weigh and no photo shows the weight, do not ask him for
grams again. Offer three sizes with their grams and kcal from the catalog value,
let him pick one, then log it `estimated: true`.

- Small (starter or tasting portion): about 100 g
- Medium (side or shared plate): about 150 g
- Large (main portion): about 200 g

State the basis in the reply, for example "324 kcal per 100 g, catalog value
for hummus", so he can see where the number comes from.
